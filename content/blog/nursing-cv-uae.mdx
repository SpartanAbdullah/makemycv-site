**Short answer: a UAE nursing or healthcare CV must lead with your licensing status, not just your experience.** UAE health authorities and hospital recruiters look first for your **licence** (DHA for Dubai, DOH for Abu Dhabi, or MOHAP, the federal ministry; active or in progress), your **DataFlow** primary-source verification status, and your **BLS/ACLS/PALS** certifications with issuers and expiry dates. Put those at the top, then structure clinical experience by specialty. This is what gets a healthcare CV shortlisted in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

## First, Know Which Authority Licenses You

Nurses are licensed by the health authority where they will work, not once for the whole country. This is what each authority's own page says (checked 21 November 2026):

| Authority | Where | What its own page says |
|---|---|---|
| **DHA** (Dubai Health Authority) | Dubai | You obtain a registration through the Sheryan portal, and the hiring healthcare facility activates it into a licence ([DHA Sheryan FAQ](https://services.dha.gov.ae/sheryan/wps/portal/home/faq)) |
| **DOH** (Department of Health, previously known as HAAD) | Abu Dhabi | Healthcare professionals must be licensed by DOH to practise in Abu Dhabi ([DOH Healthcare Professionals Manual](https://www.doh.gov.ae/-/media/A47D9907918943438EDB512BE9347AB8.ashx)) |
| **MOHAP** (Ministry of Health and Prevention) | Federal authority | Runs its own licensing service, which medical facilities apply through ([MOHAP licensing service](https://mohap.gov.ae/en/w/licensing-or-re-licensing-of-health-professional)) |

If you are not sure which authority applies, ask the employer which one its facility falls under, and do not write one authority's licence as if it covered another emirate.

The authorities' unified qualification requirements (April 2025) say the documents required for licensing are verified directly with the primary source, and DHA, DOH and MOHAP each name **DataFlow** as a verification agency. The same document lists a bachelor's degree in nursing for the Registered Nurse title ([Unified Healthcare Professional Qualification Requirements, PDF](https://mohap.gov.ae/documents/20117/0/Unified+Healthcare+Professional+Qualification+Requirements.pdf/0c6315e3-d57d-81b5-9f16-6cd7f5ac9a1a)). Your CV should signal where you are in this process.

## Put Licensing at the Top of Your CV

Right after your header, add a short **Licensing & Verification** block so a recruiter sees eligibility in seconds:

> **Licensing & Verification**
> • DHA registration issued (licence to be activated by employer) · DataFlow verification completed (Oct 2026)
> • BLS (AHA, valid to 2027) · ACLS (AHA, valid to 2027)
> • Registered Nurse — [home-country council], [registration no.]

If a facility has already activated your licence, say so plainly ("DHA-licensed Registered Nurse"). If you hold a registration or are mid-process, name the stage ("DHA registration issued", "DataFlow verification in progress"). Recruiters plan around this.

## Recommended Structure for a UAE Healthcare CV

1. **Header** — name, target role (e.g. "Registered Nurse — ICU"), city, +971 number, email, and **visa status**.
2. **Licensing & verification** — as above (DHA/DOH/MOHAP status + DataFlow + life-support certs).
3. **Professional summary** — 3–4 lines: title, years, specialty, and one measurable strength.
4. **Clinical experience** — reverse-chronological, grouped or labelled **by specialty** (e.g. ICU, ER, Med-Surg), with patient load, unit size, and outcomes.
5. **Education** — nursing degree/diploma, institution, year.
6. **Certifications & training** — BLS/ACLS/PALS/NRP, specialty courses, with issuer and expiry.
7. **Skills & systems** — clinical skills plus any EMR/HIS you've used (e.g. Cerner, Malaffi, Salama).
8. **Languages** — English is essential; Arabic is a strong plus.

## Make Clinical Experience Recruiter-Ready

Turn duties into evidence. Every role should show scale and outcomes:

- ❌ "Responsible for patient care in ICU."
- ✅ "Managed a 6-bed ICU assignment on rotating shifts; maintained zero central-line infections over 12 months; precepted 3 new graduate nurses."

Numbers a healthcare recruiter values: bed/patient load, unit size, shift type, specialty procedures, audit or safety metrics, and EMR systems used. The [nurse CV example](/cv-examples-uae/nurse) has five more rewrites, from triage to documentation.

## Keep It ATS-Friendly

Hospital groups screen high volumes through applicant tracking systems. Keep the CV single-column, use standard headings, save as a text-based **PDF**, and mirror keywords from the job posting (specialty, certifications, licence type). Write the licence the way the advert does: DOH was previously known as HAAD, so if the advert uses the old name, include both forms once. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What licence do I need to work as a nurse in the UAE?

It depends on where the facility is. The Dubai Health Authority (DHA) licenses health professionals in Dubai and the Department of Health (DOH) licenses them in Abu Dhabi. The Ministry of Health and Prevention (MOHAP) is the federal authority and runs its own licensing service. Ask the employer which authority its facility falls under.

### What is DataFlow and should it go on my CV?

DataFlow is the verification agency that DHA, DOH and MOHAP name on their own pages for checking your documents with the original source before licensing. Yes: noting your status (completed or in progress, with the month) tells recruiters how close you are to being able to start.

### What should a UAE nursing CV lead with?

Your licensing and verification status (DHA/DOH/MOHAP + DataFlow) and life-support certifications, followed by specialty-organised clinical experience. Eligibility comes before experience for regulated healthcare roles.

### Do I need the exam passed before applying?

Not necessarily. State exactly where you are: verification in progress, exam booked, exam passed or registration issued. In Dubai, the DHA describes two steps: you obtain a registration, and the hiring facility activates it into a licence, so the last step needs an employer in any case.

### How long should a healthcare CV be in the UAE?

One to two pages. Lead with licensing and your strongest specialty experience; don't pad it to three pages.
